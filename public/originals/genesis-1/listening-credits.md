# Reading and singing choices

The Bible choice uses the complete text of the selected Genesis chapter of the public-domain **Berean Standard Bible**, verses 1–31. The song-lyrics choice uses Bible Songs' original adapted lyrics for “Let There Be Light.” These are different texts and are labelled separately.

In Genesis 1, every current vocal identity has all four combinations: Bible reading, Bible singing, song-lyrics reading and song-lyrics singing. Reading is plain synthetic speech without music or singing pitch changes. Bible singing is a new original melodic setting of the chapter's words, with original synthesized accompaniment; its words are not the adapted song lyrics. The recordings are machine-generated and experimental.

The current Kokoro profiles are am_michael (Voice 1), af_heart (Voice 2), am_fenrir (Voice 3), am_onyx (Voice 4), and af_bella (Voice 5). Earlier Voice 3 and 4 recordings used adapted Matcha-TTS profiles. Their original recordings and credit files are retained for the LibriTTS-R and VCTK attributions. Kokoro's model is Apache-2.0; eSpeak and local Praat/Parselmouth are free software. No model libraries, source corpus clips or model weights are included in this app, which distributes generated audio.

The complete Bible's text is from [Berean Standard Bible](https://berean.bible/terms.htm) and is public domain. Local changes consist of speech synthesis, original melody/accompaniment, phoneme/voicing-aware timing and pitch processing, and AAC encoding. No third-party song recordings, named-artist imitation or paid generation services were used.

Genesis 2–10 readings use the official [Berean Standard Bible USJ download](https://bereanbible.com/bsb_usj.zip). Each of the five Kokoro profiles reads the verses with the original punctuation. Sentence cues seek within the spoken audio and are independent of its phrasing.
