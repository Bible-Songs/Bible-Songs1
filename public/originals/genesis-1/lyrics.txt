LET THERE BE LIGHT
Original song adaptation inspired by Genesis 1
Not a verbatim Bible translation.

Day one · Light
Before the dawn, before the day,
The earth lay dark, without a form.
God’s Spirit moved above the deep;
God spoke, and light began to dawn.

Day two · Sky
God spoke, and stretched the sky above,
Between the waters, wide and high.
The evening passed, the morning came;
A second day beneath the sky.

Refrain
Let there be light, let there be light;
The Maker speaks, the world awakes.
And all He made was very good;
We sing the wonder of His works.

Day three · Land and life
The waters drew to seas below;
The dry land rose where grass would grow.
Each tree bore fruit, each plant its seed;
God saw the good in all that grew.

Day four · Sun, moon and stars
The greater light would rule the day;
The lesser light would rule the night.
The stars would shine, the seasons turn;
God filled the heavens with their light.

Refrain
Let there be light, let there be light;
The Maker speaks, the world awakes.
And all He made was very good;
We sing the wonder of His works.

Day five · Sea and sky
The waters teemed with living things;
The birds took flight across the sky.
God blessed their life to fill the seas,
To spread their wings and multiply.

Day six · Life on earth
God made the creatures of the earth,
And in His image, woman, man.
He blessed them, gave the earth to tend,
And plants for food across the land.

Refrain
Let there be light, let there be light;
The Maker speaks, the world awakes.
And all He made was very good;
We sing the wonder of His works.
