# Reading and singing choices

The Bible choice uses the complete Genesis 1 text of the public-domain **Berean Standard Bible**, verses 1–31. The song-lyrics choice uses Bible Songs' original adapted lyrics for “Let There Be Light.” These are different texts and are labelled separately.

Every current vocal identity has all four combinations: Bible reading, Bible singing, song-lyrics reading and song-lyrics singing. Reading is plain synthetic speech without music or singing pitch changes. Bible singing is a new original melodic setting of the chapter's words, with original synthesized accompaniment; its words are not the adapted song lyrics. The recordings are machine-generated and experimental.

The original voice identities are retained: Classic uses local eSpeak en-us+f3; Warm uses the Kokoro standard af_heart profile; voice 3 uses the locally fine-tuned Matcha-TTS model and a 124-profile composite; voice 4 uses the Matcha model and eight adapted profiles averaged together. See the separate voice 3 and voice 4 credit files for the speech-model and CC BY 4.0 LibriTTS-R/VCTK attributions. Kokoro's model is Apache-2.0; eSpeak and local Praat/Parselmouth are free software. No model libraries, source corpus clips or model weights are included in this app, which distributes generated audio.

The complete Bible's text is from [Berean Standard Bible](https://berean.bible/terms.htm) and is public domain. Local changes consist of speech synthesis, original melody/accompaniment, phoneme/voicing-aware timing and pitch processing, and AAC encoding. No third-party song recordings, named-artist imitation or paid generation services were used.
