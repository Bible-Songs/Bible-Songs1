# Scripture choice for Bible Songs — 7 November 2026

## Recommendation

Berean Standard Bible (BSB), selected as the best overall fit for an NIV reader among the alternatives reviewed. This is an editorial judgment based on sampled wording, approach and reuse rights, not a statistical claim that BSB is the closest translation at every verse. No exhaustive full-Bible similarity analysis was performed.

## Comparison

- **BSB:** complete modern-English Bible; its publisher places it near the middle of literal and dynamic translation approaches. Sampled passages preserve much of NIV's familiar sentence structure, with some more traditional vocabulary. Public domain; all uses freely permitted, including free/commercial adaptation. Best overall combination for this project.
- **World English Bible Classic:** public domain and complete. Its Genesis 1 wording sometimes matches NIV closely, but has a more traditional/literal cadence in other places. Less suited overall to the requested reading style.
- **Free Bible Version:** modern and conversational; in places more colloquial than NIV. Freely reusable under CC BY-SA 4.0, requiring attribution, change notices and share-alike licensing for covered adaptations. A viable option, but carries obligations for adapted lyrics.
- **Open English Bible:** modern, CC0/public domain; Genesis is available, but the project has not completed the entire Old Testament. Its Genesis phrasing and verse layout differ more substantially in the reviewed passage. Less suitable for a planned complete Bible-song library.

Reviewed Genesis 1 in all four alternatives and NIV, plus the publisher's BSB/NIV comparison including Psalm 23:4, Psalm 139:14, Proverbs 3:6, Isaiah 53:5, Matthew 6:9, Romans 8:28 and 2 Timothy 3:16. Differences remain: in Genesis 1:2 BSB uses “formless and void”; verse 6 uses “expanse”; verse 26 uses “man”. BSB is not NIV, nor its 2011 gender-language policy.

## Implementation

The complete reference chapter is BSB, with verse numbers 1–31 and source/terms links. Footnotes, section headings and cross-references are excluded from verse text. Song lyrics remain an original Genesis 1 adaptation, not a verbatim Bible translation or a song branded as an official BSB edition. No third-party audio was reintroduced.
